What the league is

Conquerors of Wraeclast is the launch league of Path of Exile 2 1.0, starting on 11 December 2026. It adds a strategy layer on top of the game: you recruit an army, build a castle and lay siege to enemy strongholds. Before each battle you place your troops, then fight alongside them.

1.0 also brings the final two acts of the campaign and two new classes.

The league mechanic

In the campaign Acts I–VI + Interludes

  • How it starts. Mercenaries who deserted the Count ask you to help free the rest of their band.
  • Points of interest. More than 70 new league points of interest along the campaign.
  • Battles. 23 battles where your troops fight with you.

In the endgame Atlas

  • 100 castles. One shared map outside the starting area of the Atlas, with factions fighting for control of the castles.
  • Commanders. Commanders such as Athela can run maps for you while you are away.
  • Rewards. Major league bosses, such as Valen, the Dread Carnifex, drop Treasury keys and a new crafting material for reforging items.

Until 11 December: Forbidden Rites Patch 0.5.5 · live now

Forbidden Rites is the event league running until 1.0. Its mechanic is a reworked Ritual, with a campaign half and an endgame half.

In the campaign Acts I–IV + Interludes

  • One Ritual in every area. Every campaign zone holds a Ritual encounter that rewards levelling items: crafting currency and a batch of equipment.
  • Boss Ritual clusters. Twice per Act and once per Interlude, clusters of Ritual Effigies appear on the World Map; each boss you beat is carried into the next Ritual of the cluster.
  • Chain payout. Finish the series and spend your accumulated Tribute on Unique Items.
  • Aoife introduces each encounter; in a party, every player in the area must hear her before the Ritual starts.

In the endgame Atlas · Maps

  • Sacred Bloom adds the Viridian Wildwood to a cluster of Maps: a fog-shrouded forest where you collect trails of Azmeri Wisps.
  • Wisps empower the map: monsters become more powerful and more rewarding, depending on the Wisps you gathered.
  • Stacking Tablets add an extra Altar where possible and more Wisps in the Wildwood.
  • Royal Lenience lets offered Tribute Defer items at 500% more cost; party members earn their own Tribute.
UnverifiedThe Wisp types (Primal, Vivid, Wild, and a possible Sacred Wisp) are community-derived from PoE1's Affliction league and are not named in the 0.5.5 notes.

Runes of Aldur, the league before, still runs alongside it. Much of it went core in 0.5.5: Farrow, the Verisium Anvil (unlocked in Act IV) and its Expedition encounters. In the act guides, Runes of Aldur notes sit in burgundy boxes; the League notes button shows or hides them.

Which League This Guide Covers 0.5.5 · Forbidden Rites

This site always tracks the most recent live league — currently 0.5.5 Forbidden Rites. Everything here is written for that league's rules, pathing, and rewards. It is not tailored to Standard, and league-specific mechanics will not exist on a Standard character.

Forbidden Rites is an event league with its own economy and ladder, running until the 1.0 release. The previous league, Runes of Aldur, continues to run alongside it — and much of its content (Farrow, the Verisium Anvil, Expedition encounters) went core in 0.5.5, so that part now applies everywhere, Standard included.

Reconciliation in progress. The 0.5.5 patch notes have landed and the guides are being updated against them — Forbidden Rites Ritual encounters, the Trial of Chaos rework, and the Runes of Aldur content that went core. Pages not yet fully reconciled still describe 0.5 rules. Cross-check the official patch notes before anything expensive.
